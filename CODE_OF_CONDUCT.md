# Code of conduct

Chainwake contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation safe,
welcoming, and productive.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Welcome differing experience levels and technical viewpoints.
- Give feedback about work, not people.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's confidential information.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, sustained disruption,
and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `hello@blockmachine.io`. Do not use a
public issue. Include relevant context and links while minimizing sensitive
personal information.

Maintainers will review reports privately and may remove content, issue a
warning, restrict participation, or permanently exclude a participant,
depending on severity and pattern. Retaliation against a reporter or witness is
not tolerated.

This code applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing
the project.
